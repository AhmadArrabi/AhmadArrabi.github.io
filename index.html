Ahmad Arrabi

PhD student at UVM

ahmad.arrabi@uvm.edu

I am a 3rd year computer science Ph.D. student at the university of vermont, working with Prof. Safwan Wshah. My research interest is in generative models and cross-view synthesis. I also do some applied work, mainly in medical imaging and remote sensing. Currenlty, I am more focused on score based diffusion models and their applications.

I always find joy in research and discovery, don’t hesitate to reach out for collaboration! 😁
